How do I know what size solar system I need?

What is Net Metering and does Solarix support it?

Net Metering is a NEPRA-approved utility billing mechanism that credits you for the surplus electricity your solar panels export back to the national grid. For example, during sunny days when production exceeds your consumption, the meter runs backward. Our **10KW Premium Powerhouse Package** includes full documentation, utility approvals, three-phase connection upgrades, and NEPRA registration handled entirely by our engineering team.

What is the difference between On-Grid, Off-Grid, and Hybrid systems?
  • On-Grid Systems: Feed excess power directly to the national grid via net-metering. They are cost-effective but turn off automatically during utility blackouts/load shedding for grid safety.
  • Off-Grid Systems: Run completely independent of the utility company, using a battery bank to store all solar energy. They do not feed power back to the grid.
  • Hybrid Systems: The optimal choice for Pakistan. They support net metering to sell excess power *and* incorporate battery backup to keep your air conditioners, lights, and appliances running smoothly during load shedding.
